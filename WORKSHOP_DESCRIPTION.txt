[h1]B47ANCE'd Earthworks[/h1]
[b]Mod Version: 0.18.0[/b] - development source; native acceptance pending
Lua-only runtime. Requires B47ANCED Knowledge & Wisdom (B47ANCEDKnowledgeAndWisdom) and CAT Construction (CATConstruction). Uses vanilla rendering without custom cutaways or a retained surface view while underground.
Dig, deepen and frame trenches, moats and basements using native tools and CAT machines. Concrete roads require CAT; gravel paths remain hand-buildable.
CAT manuals grant machine operation, repair and maintenance knowledge through B47ANCED Knowledge & Wisdom. They appear in general loot and once in matching CAT vehicle gloveboxes.
Collect excavation spoil in sacks, transport it by hand, CAT cargo or experimental wheelbarrow, and return it for backfill. Includes owned worn-ground overlays and optional weatherWorks rain cleaning and itjustWorks heavy-object transactions.
The wheelbarrow is an experimental native pushable with placeholder art and single-player pushing only. Save/reload, collision, visual and multiplayer acceptance remain user-run release gates. This is not B47ANCE'd Earthworks 1.0.
Load dragged human and zombie bodies into the wheelbarrow and unload them back to native dragging.
