[h1]B47ANCE'd Earthworks[/h1]
[b]Mod Version: 0.19.1[/b] - development MP test build
Standalone Lua-only excavation and construction with vanilla tools. No required external mods or bundled vehicle adapters.
Dig, frame, deepen, climb and backfill trenches, moats and basements. Move spoil in native sacks/containers and build gravel paths. Server validates shared gameplay.
Uses vanilla rendering; fluid simulation, custom cutaways and retained underground surface views are unavailable. Experimental wheelbarrow pushing remains SP-only and unavailable in dedicated MP.
MP testing build - dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED. Dedicated server/two clients is the first native target; SP is optional. G7 and stable-release G8 remain BLOCKED pending runtime evidence.
